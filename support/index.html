Include safely

Include the app version, the operation being attempted, and any redacted error message. Never send a Pinterest password, App secret, access token, refresh token, authorization code, cookie, or unredacted account payload.

Local reset

The product provides separate local controls to disconnect the connected Pinterest account or remove the complete local operator configuration. These controls do not delete Pinterest content unless the user separately selects and confirms that exact action.
